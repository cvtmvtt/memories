Steganography ByteMixing

Steganography ByteMixing is an experimental technique that involves embedding one image into another using steganography and then intentionally mis-decoding it with the wrong key, format, or parameters to produce visually glitched or corrupted results. Unlike traditional steganography, which focuses on hidden and accurate data retrieval, ByteMixing embraces the aesthetic of distortion, using data misinterpretation as a creative tool to generate unique, chaotic, and abstract visual effects.
